Mizpah Ad Blocker

Blocks ads, trackers, pop-ups and cookie banners in Safari. It collects no data.

Turn it on

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps › Safari › Extensions.
  3. Tap Mizpah Ad Blocker and turn on Allow Extension.
  4. Set All Websites to Allow so it can block ads everywhere.

Open Safari, tap aA in the address bar, then tap Manage Extensions to check that Mizpah is on. Tap Mizpah Ad Blocker there to see what was blocked or to pause it on a site.

Turn on Mizpah in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

Mizpah is on in Safari.

Mizpah is off. Turn it on in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

  1. Tick Mizpah Ad Blocker in Safari Settings › Extensions.
  2. Click Edit Websites… and set Other Websites to Allow.

Mizpah quits when Safari Settings opens.
