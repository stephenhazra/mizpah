Mizpah Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Mizpah does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your device, and nothing it keeps ever leaves it.

What Mizpah does on your device

Mizpah checks the web addresses your browser requests against filter lists, so it can block ads, trackers and pop-ups. It also looks at the page structure to hide ad elements. This happens entirely inside your browser. No browsing data, page content or web address is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

What Mizpah stores

Mizpah keeps the following in your browser's extension storage, on your device only:

  • Your settings: trusted sites, sites where all pop-ups are blocked, your own filters and which lists are turned on.
  • Up to 1,000 site names learned from pop-ups Mizpah closed, each forgotten after 30 days.
  • Counts of blocked pop-ups.
  • The downloaded filter lists.
  • While your browser is open, the names of sites you recently opened yourself, so Mizpah never learns them as pop-up sites. This list is held in memory only and is cleared when the browser closes.

None of this leaves your device unless you export your settings yourself with the backup feature. Settings › Clear learned data removes the learned sites, the recent-sites list and the counts at any time, and uninstalling Mizpah removes everything.

Network requests Mizpah makes

Once a day, Mizpah downloads updated public filter lists from their publishers: EasyList and EasyPrivacy from easylist.to, and Fanboy's Annoyance list from secure.fanboy.co.nz. These are plain downloads sent without cookies, identifiers or a referrer, and they contain no information about your browsing. Like any web request, the list publishers' servers can see your IP address and browser version. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings.

No analytics, no accounts, no ads

Mizpah has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, user accounts or advertising. It does not use remote code.

Permissions

Mizpah needs access to all websites so it can filter ads wherever you browse. It uses that access only to block and hide ads, trackers and pop-ups as described above.
